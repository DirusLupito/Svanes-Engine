The source of the terrain is https://oceania.generic-mapping-tools.org/server/earth/earth_relief/earth_relief_15s_p/
The source of the water mask is https://d3ei3ebu1iflov.cloudfront.net/gmt-data/data/server/earth/earth_mask/earth_mask_15s_p.grd
The water mask samples the GMT GSHHG Earth Mask at each elevation cell's center. Even shoreline levels are water (1) and odd levels are land (0). GSHHG by Paul Wessel and Walter H. F. Smith is available under the GNU Lesser General Public License. https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.7007502
